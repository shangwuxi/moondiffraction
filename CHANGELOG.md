# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial independent reciprocal diffraction kernel: validated metric/site models, complete budgeted Miller enumeration, compensated complex structure factors, seven translational centerings, Bragg accessibility, powder grouping and normalized Gaussian sampling, ambiguous peak matching, zone laws, analytic sensitivities, numeric CSV/versioned JSON, three runnable workflows and independent Cartesian oracle.

Not a rewrite of MoonINI and not a CIF or full physical diffraction engine. MoonCakes publication is separate from this source release.
